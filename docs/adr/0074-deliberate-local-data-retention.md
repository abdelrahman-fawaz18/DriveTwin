# ADR 0074. Deliberate local data retention

Date: November 18, 2026. Status: approved for the pre-v1 cleanup.

Keep five newest ordinary recorded user sessions and their usable replay and backup data. Additional protected material includes active or unresolved recovery state, current scenario and elevation inputs, original frozen models and consumed evaluation identities, complete named release references, and the original matched demonstration recordings, protocol, and export audits.

Obsolete ordinary results and their backups may be removed together after identifying their consumers. Keep compact summaries of retired runs, including failed and incomplete outcomes. Preserve source and configuration identities; retirement does not produce new evidence or expand a historical claim. This supersedes the earlier blanket keep-all rule for deliberate cleanup.

Use exact, reviewed file inventories and sequential deletion inside the canonical workspace. Check current ownership immediately before removal and refuse redirected paths. Reconcile saved-session indexes and current references. Do not remove active credentials, leases, unresolved recovery resources, or host storage. No global Docker pruning, fleet workload, model retuning, deployment, or publication is authorized by this decision.

Automatic retention and the optional bounded-storage profile remain disabled. [Procedure](../setup/local-data-retention.md) · [Privacy](../privacy.md)
