# Local simulation capabilities and evidence

The linked reports retain their original test conditions, source fingerprints, and outcomes. Reports whose raw payloads were retired are summary-only. See the [retention policy](../adr/0075-minimal-historical-retention.md) and [known issues and fixes](../setup/known-issues-and-fixes.md).

DriveTwin supports one simulated bus at a time in explicitly configured local profiles. The current geographic launcher uses the route-aware physics predictor when Energy Forecast is selected. Earlier release gates and model studies keep their original source revisions, inputs, and evaluation identities. This matrix identifies each capability's evidence without combining distinct qualifications.

| Capability | Supported behavior | Evidence and practical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Monitoring and History | Twenty standard signals, quality and age, map, all-signal History selection, summaries, JSON/CSV exports | [Original gates Q-01–Q-06](verification.md); [current dashboard review](../verification/energy-dashboard.md). One-bus results do not establish fleet throughput. |
| Durable delivery and recovery | Original SQLite packets, MQTT retries, transactional PostgreSQL receipts, backlog reconciliation, independent restart checks | [Five-minute outage](../verification/outage-delivery-compatibility.json), [restart matrix](../verification/service-restart-status.json), and [gates Q-02, Q-03, Q-07, Q-08](verification.md). Numerical timings belong to the measured runs. |
| Replay and restoration | Scoped read credentials, independent playback clock, recording backups, isolated restore and current-reader checks | [Restoration](../verification/restoration-review.json), [release safety review](../verification/release-safety-review.json), and [procedure](../setup/history-query-and-restore.md). Private recordings and matching images are separate prerequisites. |
| Geographic Energy Forecast | Remaining roads, elevation, turns and stops; constrained movement; energy accounts, arrival/minimum charge, causal comparisons, durable single-worker scheduling | [Route-aware qualification](../verification/energy-route-aware.md), [geographic verification](../verification/energy-geographic.md). Ten real-OSM journeys use the same model without route-specific training. The final 70-forecast batch is regression evidence against SUMO battery readings, separate from frozen evaluations. |
| Route-aware Energy interface | Saved forecast-to-route/readings navigation; current, reserve, infeasible and unavailable states; uncapped History signal selection | [Dashboard acceptance](../verification/energy-dashboard-acceptance.json). Its connected check covered 135 twenty-signal messages and ten forecast jobs; earlier checks retain their sources. |
| Local launch workspace | Prepared trips, compatible services, 0.5×–20× pace, pause/resume, saved sessions, replay handoff, graceful stop and recovery | [Launcher review](../verification/launch-app.md), [pacing qualification](../verification/launch-pacing.md). Default pace is 1×; achieved pace depends on the host. A compatible installed catalog is required. |
| Moving-bus charging advice | Route-aware driving-leg estimates, station reachability, reserve and availability checks | [Operating scope](../verification/moving-bus-charging.md#operating-scope). Advice was checked on ten launcher configurations at 1× with matching assignments. |
| Operator charging | Reviewed bay and target, expiring authorization, durable grant, separate acknowledgment and observed charging/motion | [Moving-bus evidence](../verification/moving-bus-charging.md), [operator guide](../setup/moving-bus-charging.md). Ordinary approval is limited to the five named reviewed profiles and one currently available stop. |
| Automatic charging and outage replacement | One-use grants, waiting and charging, revalidation or safe stop after station failure | [Dedicated one-bus cases](../verification/moving-bus-charging.md). The 11% planning floor belongs to Ottawa and Burnaby trial conditions; these cases do not authorize general autonomous operation. |
| Guarded speed commands | Scoped requests, expiry, durable reservation, cancellation/replacement, restart fencing, independently observed effect | [Command closeout](../verification/command-acceptance.md). Acknowledgment alone does not establish an effect. |
| Thermal studies | Laboratory winding-temperature prediction; separate synthetic cooling-fault and intervention studies | [Thermal closeout](../verification/thermal-acceptance.md). Bench accuracy does not qualify simulated bus temperatures or a physical bus controller. |
| Recorded browser demo | One charging decision, two matched recorded outcomes, nine views, causal playback | [Demo verification](../verification/browser-demo.md). A semantic export of retained simulation recordings, with no live vehicle or new accuracy claim. |
| Legacy known-route energy and stationary charging | Frozen numerical/template models, historical readers, consumed evaluations and matching qualification tools | [Legacy example](../engineering/legacy-energy-example.md), [advisory closeout](../verification/energy-advisory-acceptance.md), [stationary charging closeout](../verification/stationary-charging-acceptance.md). Their learned template and reserved-trip results do not describe the route-aware predictor. |

The original [simulation profile](../../qualification/release/simulation.json) and twelve-gate [release record](verification.md) remain unchanged. Additional capability evidence supplements that record within its named conditions; it does not change what an older source-bound gate measured.

Physical hardware, fleet endurance and capacity, production deployment, adaptive parameter promotion, and automatic background retention deletion remain outside the release. Optional bounded storage stays disabled.

Source preparation distinguishes tracked inputs from installed geographic catalogs, private recordings and frozen study artifacts. Follow the [source-package procedure](source-package.md) before making clean-checkout claims. Public hosting or GitHub publication requires separate approval of the exact artifact and destination.
